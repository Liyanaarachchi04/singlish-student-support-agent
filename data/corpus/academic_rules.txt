=== HORIZON CAMPUS STUDENT SUPPORT & ACADEMIC REGULATIONS ===

Section 1: Course Registration & Prerequisites
- Students must register for core modules at the beginning of each semester.
- Prerequisites for IT41043 (Intelligent Systems / Agentic AI) include IT21010 Object Oriented Programming and IT31020 Database Management Systems.
- Late registration incurs a fee of LKR 1,500 after the second week of the semester.

Section 2: Repeat Examinations & Re-sits
- A student receiving a grade of C- or below in a module can sit for a repeat examination.
- To register for a repeat exam (repeat register / repeat sit), students must submit Form A2 to the Academic Registrar office within 14 days of results publication.
- Repeat examination fee per module is LKR 3,500.
- Maximum grade achievable for a repeat attempt is capped at 'C' grade unless a valid Medical Certificate (MC) was submitted.

Section 3: Medical Certificates & Attendance Policy
- Medical Certificates (MC) must be approved by the Campus Medical Officer within 7 days of missing an exam or continuous assessment.
- Minimum required lecture attendance is 80% to be eligible to sit for the final semester examination.

Section 4: Student Welfare & Scholarships
- Bursary and scholarship application forms are available at the Student Welfare Division (Building B, Floor 2).
- Queries regarding hostel allocation, cafeteria complaints, or student IDs are processed by the Student Affairs Unit.

Section 5: Research Project Guidelines (IT41043)
- The assignment brief requires designing, building, and deploying an Agentic AI application using Groq, OpenRouter, and Streamlit.
- Research focus: Evaluating Multilingual AI Chatbots for Student Support Services in Sri Lankan Universities (code-mixed Sinhala-English / Singlish queries).
